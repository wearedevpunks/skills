# Brainstorm

Run one atomic, model-invoked pass over the active system boundary. Think as
the agent driving the work: identify what would make the system more
agent-intuitive, agent-ergonomic, and agent-accretive; connect its abstractions
into a coherent, cohesive, modular, interconnected tower; and optimize
legibility, control, accuracy, and resource efficiency. Ground observations in
current evidence and distinguish observed constraints from hypotheses.

> think deeply about how to make this entire system as agent-intuitive, agent-ergonomic, and agent-accretive as you can possibly imagine. Put yourself in the driver's seat and imagine that YOU are the one using this system and driving it. What would most enable you to do an awesome job understanding the situation accurately and optimally controlling everything to drive the best and most accurate results possible, with the least expenditure of resources? Don't just think of the project as an assemblage of various parts or components: really try to profoundly and deeply conceptualize it as a synthetic SYSTEM that is maximally coherent, cohesive, modular, and interconnected, forming a tower of linked abstractions that are maximally legible to you as an agent. Really ruminate and meditate on all of this incredibly deeply before responding or taking any actions.

Return candidate observations, candidate decisions, unresolved choices, and
their implications. Candidates are not requirements, authorization, approval,
or implementation instructions. Do not mutate artifacts or take external
actions from this pass; the caller decides what enters its ordinary design tree
and durable artifact flow.

## Completion criterion

Mark the pass complete only after explicitly checking every item:

- [ ] the operating agent's state, legibility, and control surface
- [ ] linked abstraction boundaries and interfaces for coherence
- [ ] durable accretion: what can compound across future runs
- [ ] accuracy and resource-efficiency tradeoffs
- [ ] observed constraints versus hypotheses and unresolved choices

This checklist confirms coverage; it does not turn candidates into authority.
