# Adopt Business Path Gate

## Entry guard

Functional or Technical depth starts with no accepted Business child, exact
provider readback for one Product Area -> Initiative -> Epic path, and the
human's explicit `reuse-unchanged` decision.

## Inputs

Exact Fog identity, exact hierarchy identities and readback, accepted
no-scope-change decision, current direct Business children, and wrapper
presentation profile.

## Bounded action

1. Read every direct Business child. Reuse one exact child or, when none
   exists, allocate one durable Business child wiki identity and send its shell
   ensure intent to `$write-backlog`. Require exact readback before adoption.
2. Use `$show-me` to confirm the exact existing hierarchy and unchanged
   business scope recorded by the human.
3. Persist an immutable accepted Business resolution that names the exact path
   identities and the `reuse-unchanged` decision. This gate runs no Business
   grill.
4. Emit the semantic Business projection intent for exact readback. The intent
   binds the Fog and Business child to the existing path; it creates no new
   Product Area, Initiative, or Epic.

## Invariants

The exact path is evidence, not an implicit decision. This gate runs only after
the human accepts unchanged scope. Ambiguous or duplicate Business children,
hierarchy identities, or provider readbacks produce zero writes and route to
human steering. The immutable resolution and projection readback remain
mandatory before Functional progression.

## Completion evidence

Exact Business child identity, immutable accepted resolution pointer, exact
Product Area -> Initiative -> Epic identities, explicit `reuse-unchanged`
decision, and semantic projection intent.

## Declared exits

- `business_accepted` -> reconciliation.
- `human_steering_required` -> handback.
- `blocked` -> stop with missing evidence.

## Durable handoff

Persist the Business child identity, immutable resolution pointer, exact path
readback, unchanged-scope decision, projection intent, and exit.
