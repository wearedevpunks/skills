# Research Support Gate

## Entry guard

The active grilling child records one precise unknown answerable by readonly
investigation and no fresh retained answer exists.

## Inputs

Exact Fog, supported grilling child identity, precise question, scope, and
prior research children/evidence.

## Bounded action

Reuse or semantically request one direct Fog Research child through
`$write-backlog`, linked to the grilling child it supports. Run
`$parallel-research` in durable-report mode. Require the retained report's
immutable commit SHA and path, then route to reconciliation.

## Invariants

Research reports facts and cannot accept product direction or authorize backlog
projection.

## Completion evidence

Stable Research child identity, supported-child relation, immutable report SHA
and path, and exact child/relation readback.

## Declared exits

- `support_resolved` -> reconciliation then router re-entry.
- `human_steering_required` -> handback.
- `blocked` -> stop with missing retained evidence.

## Durable handoff

Persist Research identity, supported child, report pointer, readback, and exit.
