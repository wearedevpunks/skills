# Research Support Gate

## Entry guard

A named Grilling child records one precise unknown answerable by readonly
investigation and no fresh retained answer exists.

## Bounded action

Reuse an obviously relevant direct Fog Research child or create one when the
evidence supports separate useful work. Link it to the named Grilling child,
run `$parallel-research` in durable-report mode, and retain the report by
immutable commit SHA and path. Ambiguity requests human steering before
creation.

Research returns facts to its supported Grilling child. It cannot accept product direction or authorize backlog
projection independently.

## Completion evidence

Exact Research child identity, supported-child relation, report retained by
immutable commit SHA and path, and exact child/relation readback.

## Declared exits

- `support_resolved` -> router re-entry.
- `human_steering_required` -> handback.
- `blocked` -> stop with missing retained evidence.

## Durable handoff

Persist Research identity, supported Grilling child, report pointer, readback,
and exit.
